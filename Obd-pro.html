Your 2.5i has no turbo, so the turbo sound is “virtual”: spool builds from throttle and RPM, and lifting off fires the valve sound.

Wi-Fi OBD scanners

Wi-Fi ELM327 scanners need a direct network connection (usually 192.168.0.10, port 35000). Phone browsers do not allow web pages to open that kind of connection, so no web app can talk to a Wi-Fi scanner.

Options:

  • Use a Bluetooth LE scanner (Vgate iCar Pro BLE, Veepeak OBDCheck BLE, OBDLink CX). Works here with “Pair & connect”.
  • Or a native Android app version of this dashboard (installable APK) – that can use Wi-Fi and classic Bluetooth scanners.
